# Central Coast SA2 Intelligence Report

**Prepared: August 2026 | Indicators: July 2026 | Data Coverage: 30 SA2 areas, Central Coast LGA (NSW)**

## Executive Summary

The Central Coast presents a region of stark contrasts, with pockets of affluence alongside areas experiencing significant socioeconomic stress. The LGA's total population across 30 SA2 areas exceeds 300,000 residents, with notable growth concentrated in newer developments like **Warnervale-Wadalba** (+650 persons) and **Gosford-Springfield** (+516 persons). While unemployment rates vary from just **1.2%** in **Avoca Beach-Copacabana** to **8.9%** in **Wyong**, housing affordability pressures are universal, with median house prices ranging from **$820,500** to **$1,947,000**. Crime patterns reveal concerning disparities, with assault rates spanning from **0.09 per 1,000** in **Lake Munmorah-Mannering Park** to **12.36 per 1,000** in **Wyong**.

**Five Headline Findings:**

1. **Warnervale-Wadalba** leads population growth (+650, 2.9%), driven by internal migration (+469), signaling new housing development appeal
2. Affluent coastal SA2s (**Avoca Beach-Copacabana**, **Wamberal-Forresters Beach**, **Terrigal-North Avoca**) show unemployment below 2%, high education levels, and SEIFA indices exceeding 1,075
3. **Wyong** and **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven** face compound disadvantage: unemployment above 6%, indigenous populations near 6%, and elevated crime rates
4. Housing costs burden lower-income areas, with price-to-income ratios exceeding 17:1 in disadvantaged suburbs while remaining around 8-10:1 in affluent zones
5. Tourism infrastructure concentrates in **Gosford-Springfield** (46,507,000 total spend) and coastal areas, with **The Entrance** hosting 94 tourism businesses

## 1. Population & Demographics

The Central Coast's 30 SA2 areas demonstrate diverse demographic profiles. **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven** is the most populous SA2 (22,740), followed by **Bateau Bay-Killarney Vale** (22,990) and **Umina-Booker Bay-Patonga** (25,007). Population growth varies dramatically: **Warnervale-Wadalba** added 650 residents (2.9% growth), while **Toukley-Norah Head** contracted by 19 persons (-0.2%).

Overseas migration drives most growth, with **Gosford-Springfield** receiving 305 new arrivals and **Terrigal-North Avoca** gaining 224. However, **Warnervale-Wadalba** uniquely shows strong internal migration (+469), indicating domestic relocation appeal. Natural increase remains modest or negative in aging areas like **Toukley-Norah Head** (-111) and **Umina-Booker Bay-Patonga** (-86).

Median age ranges from 35 years in **Warnervale-Wadalba** and **Kariong** to 49 in **Lake Munmorah-Mannering Park** and **Erina-Green Point**. Youth populations (under 20) peak at **31.45%** in **Warnervale-Wadalba**, while seniors (over 69) concentrate in **Lake Munmorah-Mannering Park** (25.7%) and **Erina-Green Point** (26.04%).

Indigenous populations are significant in several SA2s: **Blue Haven-San Remo** (7.78%), **Wyong** (6.61%), and **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven** (5.64%), totaling 1,247, 583, and 1,247 persons respectively. Cultural diversity varies, with **Gosford-Springfield** showing the highest linguistic diversity (13.21% speak languages other than English), while rural **Lake Munmorah-Mannering Park** records just 2.63%.

Population density spans from 6.1 persons/km² in rural **Calga-Kulnura** to 2,117 persons/km² in **Bateau Bay-Killarney Vale**, reflecting the divide between coastal urbanization and hinterland acreage living.

## 2. Economic Activity & Business

Business density correlates strongly with affluence and population. **Gosford-Springfield** dominates with 2,629 total businesses, including 150 manufacturing, 335 healthcare, and 120 tourism enterprises. **Terrigal-North Avoca** hosts 1,577 businesses (202 healthcare, 93 tourism), while **Erina-Green Point** supports 1,807 businesses with strong healthcare representation (232).

Manufacturing concentrates in specific nodes: **Gosford-Springfield** (150), **Calga-Kulnura** (98), and **Chittaway Bay-Tumbi Umbi** (72). Healthcare businesses proliferate in affluent areas: **Erina-Green Point** (232), **Terrigal-North Avoca** (202), and **Wamberal-Forresters Beach** (144).

Labour force participation and unemployment reveal stark divides. **Avoca Beach-Copacabana** achieves just **1.2% unemployment** from a 4,254-person labour force, with only 130 JobSeeker recipients. **Wamberal-Forresters Beach** (1.6% unemployment) and **Terrigal-North Avoca** (1.4%) demonstrate similar strength. Conversely, **Wyong** suffers **8.9% unemployment** (392 unemployed, 615 JobSeeker), **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven** records **6.1%** (666 unemployed, 1,175 JobSeeker), and **Blue Haven-San Remo** shows **6.2%** (368 unemployed, 595 JobSeeker).

Median household income ranges from **$1,942/week** in **Jilliby-Yarramalong** and **$1,869** in **Avoca Beach-Copacabana** down to **$971** in **Toukley-Norah Head** and **$977** in **Lake Munmorah-Mannering Park**. This income disparity drives differential price-to-income ratios and quality-of-life outcomes.

## 3. Housing & Affordability

Housing markets vary enormously across the Central Coast. Premium coastal properties command extraordinary prices: **Erina-Green Point** records a median house price of **$1,947,000** (units: $2,639,000), **Wamberal-Forresters Beach** reaches **$1,746,250** (units: $1,260,000), and **Terrigal-North Avoca** sits at **$1,630,000** (units: $1,165,000).

Mid-market areas cluster around $1.0-1.2 million: **Gosford-Springfield** ($1,151,250), **Niagara Park-Lisarow** ($1,134,625), and **Chittaway Bay-Tumbi Umbi** ($1,113,750). Entry-level markets exist in **Tuggerah-Kangy Angy** ($820,500 houses) and **Blue Haven-San Remo** ($830,000 houses, $565,000 units).

Weekly rents mirror purchase prices. Premium areas demand **$850-972** weekly for houses (**Wamberal-Forresters Beach**: $850, **Box Head-MacMasters Beach**: $972.57), while affordable options exist in **Toukley-Norah Head** ($617.50), **Blue Haven-San Remo** ($611.50), and **Wyong** ($625).

Development activity concentrates in growth corridors: **Warnervale-Wadalba** approved 250 dwellings (building value: $104.1M), **Lake Munmorah-Mannering Park** approved 159 ($55.7M), and **The Entrance** approved 152 ($102.2M). **Summerland Point-Gwandalan** approved 104 dwellings ($52.1M).

Price-to-income ratios reveal affordability stress. In **Wyong**, the median house costs 17.3 times median annual household income ($903,888 ÷ $54,912), while **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven** faces 16.9:1 ($879,255 ÷ $54,652). Affluent areas show healthier ratios: **Avoca Beach-Copacabana** at 8.9:1 ($1,637,500 ÷ $97,188) and **Wamberal-Forresters Beach** at 9.5:1.

## 4. Socioeconomic Wellbeing

SEIFA indices starkly delineate advantage and disadvantage across the Central Coast. The Index of Relative Socio-economic Advantage and Disadvantage ranges from **1,085.45** in **Terrigal-North Avoca** and **1,084.48** in **Avoca Beach-Copacabana** down to **886.3** in **Blue Haven-San Remo** and **891.37** in **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven**.

Education attainment follows similar patterns. Year 12 completion peaks at **45.48%** in **Terrigal-North Avoca** and **44.53%** in **Avoca Beach-Copacabana**, contrasting with **22.92%** in **Lake Munmorah-Mannering Park** and **22.99%** in **Blue Haven-San Remo**. Bachelor degree attainment reaches **15.02%** in **Terrigal-North Avoca** and **14.74%** in **Avoca Beach-Copacabana** but falls to **3.3%** in **Blue Haven-San Remo** and **4.4%** in **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven**.

Welfare dependency concentrates in disadvantaged areas. **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven** has 3,555 Age Pensioners, 6,935 Pension Concession Card holders, 2,595 Commonwealth Rent Assistance recipients, and 1,535 Disability Support Pensioners. **Wyong** shows 1,200 Age Pensioners, 2,755 Pension Concession Card holders, and 770 Disability Support Pensioners from just 9,360 residents.

Homelessness affects 236 persons in **Wyong**, 273 in **The Entrance**, 160 in **Gosford-Springfield**, and 138 in **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven**. These four SA2s account for the majority of the region's homeless population.

## 6. Tourism & Visitor Economy

Tourism infrastructure and visitor spending concentrate in specific nodes. **Gosford-Springfield** dominates with **$46,507,000** total spend, reflecting its role as the regional hub with 120 tourism businesses. **Erina-Green Point** attracts **$68,963,000** spend (85 tourism businesses), while **Gorokan-Kanwal-Charmhaven** generates **$28,757,000** (46 businesses).

Coastal tourism hotspots include **Bateau Bay-Killarney Vale** ($23,030,000 spend, 46 businesses), **Umina-Booker Bay-Patonga** ($18,850,000 spend, 74 businesses), and **Woy Woy-Blackwall** ($17,879,000 spend, 50 businesses).

**The Entrance** hosts **94 tourism businesses** with $13,249,000 total spend, establishing it as a key visitor destination. **Terrigal-North Avoca** supports **93 tourism businesses** with $7,602,000 spend, suggesting a higher-end, lower-volume market.

Airbnb metrics (measured by listing visits per property) reveal visitor interest patterns. **Terrigal-North Avoca** leads with **2,348 visits per property**, followed by **Bateau Bay-Killarney Vale** (1,971.67) and **Tuggerah-Kangy Angy** (512, though this appears anomalously low). **Wyoming** shows extremely high visitor interest (1,847), **Ourimbah-Fountaindale** (1,827), and **Niagara Park-Lisarow** (1,508).

Rural and hinterland areas show modest tourism engagement: **Jilliby-Yarramalong** (9 businesses, $3,166,000 spend), **Calga-Kulnura** (35 businesses, $3,529,000 spend).

## 7. Regional Divide: Gosford vs Wyong

The Central Coast's two SA3 sub-regions—**Gosford** and **Wyong**—demonstrate fundamentally different socioeconomic profiles.

**Gosford** encompasses affluent coastal suburbs (**Avoca Beach-Copacabana**, **Wamberal-Forresters Beach**, **Terrigal-North Avoca**, **Erina-Green Point**) alongside the regional centre (**Gosford-Springfield**). This sub-region shows:
- Lower unemployment (typically 1.2-2.5%)
- Higher median household incomes ($1,197-1,869/week)
- Superior education outcomes (Year 12: 37-45%, Bachelor: 11-15%)
- SEIFA advantage indices above 1,020 in premium areas
- House prices $1.1M-1.9M
- Lower crime rates
- Stronger business density and healthcare infrastructure
